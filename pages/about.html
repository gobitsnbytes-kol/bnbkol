Who we are.

A teen-led code club dedicated to empowering high-agency individuals to ship production-grade technology through real-world product launches.

The Kolkata fork of bits&bytes™.

Built from the ground up.

  1. Stop 01

    The origin story

    After competing in a national-level competition and discovering the Bits&Bytes fork program, our lead realised Kolkata lacked a real platform for student builders. He forked Bits&Bytes to fill that gap, bringing world-class tech events and a high-agency culture to the City of Joy.

  2. Stop 02

    High agency only

    We move away from beginner formats that treat participants like they need hand-holding. We build for exceptionally talented people who want to ship real products, not attend lectures.

  3. Stop 03

    Ship real products

    Workshops and hack nights must convert into tangible outcomes. We focus on premium hackathons, dev squads and real-world launches, fully student-led.

  4. Stop 04

    Production grade

    We prioritise performance and stability. Our infrastructure is built to professional standards, removing barriers for the next generation of builders.
