What we look for.

For the working team.

  1. You're already building something, shipped or actively shipping.
  2. You can commit consistently, not just show up once.
  3. You take ownership. No hand-holding.
  4. You're based in, or can operate in, Kolkata.

Common questions.

Community member or working team: what's the difference?

Community members join the Discord, attend events and collaborate with other builders. No application, open to any teen. The working team is the core crew that organises and runs the fork; that's selective and needs an application.

Do I need coding experience to join the community?

No. The community is open to any teen interested in tech, at any skill level. Show up, contribute, build things.

What does the working team application involve?

A short form about what you've built, what role you want to play, and why Kolkata. We review every submission and follow up directly.

Is there a cost?

Nothing. Both tracks are free.

I'm not based in Kolkata. Can I still join?

Community: yes, fully. Working team: we need people who can be on the ground in Kolkata for events.
